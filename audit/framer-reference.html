Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, arriving, and settling in at Constellare. If your question isn’t answered here, our front desk is always a message away.

What are your check-in and check-out times?

Check-in opens at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early arrival and late departure can usually be arranged. Mention it when you book and we will hold the room wherever availability allows.

Can I change or cancel a booking after it is confirmed?

Yes. Flexible rates can be changed or cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Saver rates are non-refundable, and the exact conditions are always shown before you confirm.

How do I choose the right room or suite?

Our eight rooms and suites range from compact doubles for short city stays to garden suites built for longer visits. Every listing shows the bed configuration, view, and maximum occupancy, and our team is happy to recommend one.

Is breakfast included, and what else can I use during my stay?

Breakfast is included with every suite and can be added to any room at booking. Guests also have full access to the restaurant, bar, spa treatment rooms, and the library lounge throughout their stay.

Do you offer parking, airport transfers, and accessible rooms?

On-site parking and private airport transfers can be arranged at booking or any time before arrival. We also have step-free accessible rooms. Contact us directly and we will match you with the right one.
