## 6. Nearby Historical Attractions

The resort is within walking distance or a short rickshaw ride from Orchha's premier monuments:
1. **The Royal Chhatris (14 Cenotaphs):** 500 meters along the Betwa riverbank; magnificent 17th-century cenotaphs of Bundela kings.
2. **Orchha Fort Complex (Raja Mahal & Jahangir Mahal):** 1.2 km; breathtaking Mughal-Rajput palaces renowned for intricate frescoes and stone lattice screens.
3. **Ram Raja Temple:** 1.0 km; the only sacred shrine in India where Lord Rama is venerated as a crowned temporal monarch with police guard-of-honor.
4. **Chaturbhuj Temple:** 1.1 km; towering soaring spire resembling a cathedral, offering panoramic views of the city.
5. **Laxminarayan Temple:** 1.8 km; unique triangular fort-temple hybrid containing vibrant mural frescoes depicting historical battles.
6. **Orchha Wildlife Sanctuary & River Rafting:** 1.5 km; peaceful forest sanctuary on the Betwa island; gentle Class I–II white-water river rafting.
