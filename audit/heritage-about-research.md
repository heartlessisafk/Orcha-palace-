## 1. Research Separation & Source Integrity

In strict adherence to research integrity guidelines, all content for the Heritage & About Page (`/about`) is classified into three distinct categories:

### A. Verified Resort Facts (Source: Official Resort Documentation)
*Primary Source:* `https://www.orchharesort.com/` and `Orchha Resort Fact Sheet.....pdf` (Downloaded & preserved in `audit/orchha-subpages/`)
1. **Property Setting & Atmosphere:** Located at Kanchanghat, Distt. Tikamgarh, Orchha, Madhya Pradesh 472246, on the peaceful banks of the river Betwa. Specifically documented as a calm, serene, "100% non-polluted" sanctuary away from urban congestion.
2. **Connectivity:** Exactly 18 km from Jhansi railway station and 120 km from Gwalior airport. Well connected by road and rail from Delhi, Agra, Gwalior, Kanpur, Lucknow, and Khajuraho.
3. **Architect:** Designed by **Architect Yogesh Chandra** (New Delhi, India), utilizing low-slung pavilions and warm terracotta/stone palettes that harmonize with Bundelkhand's landscape without imitating a faux antiquity.
4. **Accommodation Inventory:** Exactly **44 guest keys**:
   - 27 Standard Rooms
   - 6 Deluxe Rooms
   - 11 Deluxe Air-Conditioned Swiss Tents
5. **Dining & Culinary:**
   - **Kaleva**: Multi-cuisine restaurant offering Indian, Continental, and Chinese cuisine.
   - **Lounge Bar & Poolside Service**: Open-air refreshments served by the pool deck.
6. **Recreation & Wellness:**
   - **Lotus-Shaped Swimming Pool**: Centrally situated pool surrounded by landscaped lawns.
   - **Kerala Ayurveda Wellness Pavilion**: Authentic oil massages (Abhyangam, Shirodhara) and herbal steam bath.
   - **Riverside Sports**: Outdoor tennis and badminton courts set adjacent to the riverbank; fitness center / health club.
   - **Evening Cultural Life**: Nightly traditional Bundelkhand folk dance performances under the open sky.
   - **Banquet Facility**: 1,500 sq ft Ballroom accommodating 70–150 guests.

### B. Verified Orchha Heritage Facts (Source: MP Tourism & Incredible India)
*Sources:* `mptourism.com/destination-orchha.php`, `incredibleindia.gov.in`, UNESCO tentative list dossier.
1. **Name Origin:** "Orchha" (or *Ondchho*) literally translates to *"Hidden"* — selected for its defensive seclusion within dense teak and dhak forests along the rocky bends of the Betwa.
2. **Founding of Orchha:** Founded in **1531 AD** by Bundela Rajput chieftain **Raja Rudra Pratap Singh**, who established the island capital surrounded by the Betwa River and began the palace and arched causeway.
3. **The 14 Royal Chhatris of Kanchan Ghat:** Located directly along the riverbank opposite and adjacent to Kanchanghat. Constructed in the 17th and 18th centuries in honor of Bundela rulers (including Madhukar Shah and Bir Singh Deo). Unlike conventional Islamic tombs, these three-storey cenotaphs synthesize Rajput cubical halls with Hindu temple *shikharas* (spires) and pillared *chhatris*.
4. **Ram Raja Temple:** The only shrine in India where Lord Rama is venerated not merely as a god, but as an active, living Sovereign King (accorded an armed police guard of honour at every Aarti).
5. **Chaturbhuj Temple & Jahangir Mahal:** Chaturbhuj boasts one of the tallest sanctum spires in central India; Jahangir Mahal was completed by Raja Bir Singh Deo (r. 1605–1627) featuring eight fluted domes, hanging jharokhas, and turquoise ceramic tilework.

### C. Creative Storytelling (Editorial Expression)
- Poetic descriptions of morning mist over the Betwa River, the evening silhouettes of the 14 cenotaphs, Bundeli hospitality, and the luxury of unhurried time.
- **Strict Guardrail:** The resort itself is never claimed to be an ancient royal palace, monument, or historical fortress. It is framed as an exquisitely designed riverfront retreat that offers contemporary royal comfort right at the doorstep of historic Orchha.
