# Phase 31 Summary — Full Hero Content Glass Backdrop

## Executive Summary
Delivered the client-requested **unified translucent glass-style background panel** behind the entire central hero content in the Orchha Resort Story / Kanchan Ghat Sanctuary hero section (`src/components/about/AboutHero.tsx`).

## Key Deliverables
1. **Unified Translucent Glass Panel**:
   - Encompasses eyebrow badge, main heading, supporting description, 3 statistic cards, and both action buttons in a single cohesive container.
   - Finished with deep charcoal/navy gradient (`from-[#070F1E]/80 via-[#0B1528]/75 to-[#070F1E]/85`), backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md backdrop-saturate-150`), `rounded-3xl`, `border-white/15`, and inner highlight ring.
2. **Harmonious Interior Elements**:
   - 3 statistic cards converted to nested frosted glass tiles (`bg-white/[0.05] border-white/12`).
   - Crisp white heading with luminous `#F3D382` golden italics.
   - Clean, readable `#F4F0E6` warm beige description copy.
3. **Background Image Atmosphere Preserved**:
   - Softened image background scrims so authentic chandeliers, teak seating, and heritage wall art glow warmly behind and around the panel.
4. **Attribution Placement**:
   - Positioned neatly below the glass panel with an understated frosted pill.
5. **Zero Regressions & Universal Responsiveness**:
   - Verified 0 horizontal overflow across Desktop (1440px), Tablet (768px), and Mobile (390px).
